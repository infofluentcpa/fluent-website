Cash Flow ForecastSee the tight weeks before they arrive.

A spreadsheet from Fluent Financials, LLC that shows cash coming in and going out, weeks ahead. It has real formulas and sample data, so you can see how it works before you put your own numbers in. Free, no sign-up, no email needed.

What it answers

  • Can I afford this? See the effect of a purchase or a hire before you commit.
  • When does cash get tight? Spot the low week while there is still time to plan for it.
  • The work is done, so where is the money? Built for the gap between finishing a job and getting paid.

Made for contractors and service businesses, and any owner who wants to see cash coming.

How to use it

Three steps, about twenty minutes.

Make your copy

Google Sheets makes a copy that is yours alone, or download the Excel file. Nobody else sees your numbers.

Put in your numbers

Replace the sample data with your opening cash, the money you expect in, and the bills you expect to pay.

Look ahead every week

Update it once a week. The low points show up early, while you can still move a payment or chase an invoice.
