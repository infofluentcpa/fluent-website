Common questions

Questions About Fractional CFO Services

What does a fractional CFO do?

The same work as a full-time CFO, for the hours your business needs: forecasting cash flow, setting budgets and targets, reviewing the numbers with you every month, and working out what a big decision, like a new hire, a price change or a loan, does to profit and cash.

What is the difference between a fractional controller and a fractional CFO?

A controller makes sure your numbers are right: the monthly close, reconciliations and financial statements you can rely on. A CFO uses those numbers to plan ahead: cash flow, forecasts, budgets, pricing, financing and growth. Fractional means you get either one for the hours you need, without a full-time hire. Many clients start with bookkeeping and a controller, and add CFO work as the business grows.

How much does a fractional CFO cost?

It depends on how much you need: how often we meet, how much forecasting and reporting is involved, and whether we also keep the books. After a 15-minute fit check we give you a clear quote before any work starts. Either way it costs a fraction of a full-time CFO's salary.

Do my books need to be in good shape first?

No. If they need catching up or cleaning up, our bookkeepers do that first, so the forecasts and reviews start from numbers you can trust.

Do you work with QuickBooks?

Yes. We set up, clean up and maintain QuickBooks, and work from it for the monthly close and reporting.

Do you work with businesses outside Maryland?

Yes. We are based in Silver Spring, in Montgomery County, Maryland, and work with businesses across Washington DC, Maryland and Virginia, in New York and New Jersey, and in other states. Most of the work happens online, and meetings are by appointment.
